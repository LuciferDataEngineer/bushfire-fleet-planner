What this means

The recommended bases reach 93.0% of people against 93.5% for the biggest cities, 96.6% of this week's heat against 9.5%, and 87.3% of the dangerous-weather record against 32.7%. At 60 minutes those recommended bases reach only 34.0% of people, while the city airports still reach 90.0%. A 90-minute flight is what lets an inland aircraft still count a capital as covered.
